Before you start

How this worksClick Open / focus demo to launch the app in a second tab. Keep this console on one screen and the demo on the other (or alt-tab). Each step below has buttons that drive the demo tab for you — switching tabs, opening flows, and even typing prompts into the Assistant. Read the 🎤 script aloud as you click.
Tip: if a click doesn't land, the demo tab may have reloaded — press Open / focus demo again, then retry the step.
1

Set the scene — the Scheduler's morning

~1 min
Say"Every shift, a staffing coordinator like Dana Reyes juggles coverage, no-show risk, overtime and patient flow — today that's mostly spreadsheets and phone calls. This is a predictive, agentic workspace that gets her one shift ahead. It runs entirely on NYC H+H's own Red Hat OpenShift AI platform on AWS — and everything here is synthetic data, no PHI."
2

Predictive dashboard

~2 min
Say"At a glance: coverage, open shifts, predicted no-shows, overtime, occupancy, predicted admits. The risk mix and the staffing-vs-demand chart are driven by real models served on KServe — not static numbers. There's also an Open in Grafana link for the ops-grade view."
3

One platform, three roles

~1 min
Say"The same system tailors itself by role. The Scheduler sees no-show risk and fills; the Approver sees PTO impact and coverage conflicts; a Provider sees their own schedule; Leadership sees department performance. Watch the workspace change."
4

No-show risk — act before they don't show

~1.5 min
Say"Each appointment gets a red / amber / green no-show score from our model, with the contributing factors and a suggested action. The scheduler can filter to RED and text reminders or hold an overbook slot — turning a reactive problem into a proactive one."
5

Smart scheduling — the drill-down

~2.5 min
Say"Booking is a guided drill-down: pick a specialty, see the doctors with their next availability, open the chosen doctor's calendar — open slots are clickable, booked slots show the patient, PTO is blocked — then assign a patient. Let me book a cardiology follow-up."
After booking, click Schedule tab — the new appointment is already there (single source of truth).
6

Cancel & re-offer the slot

~1.5 min
Say"When something cancels, the slot shouldn't go to waste. Cancelling frees it and the system immediately re-offers it to a higher-risk patient who needs an earlier visit. I'll do it from the Assistant — in plain English."
7

PTO impact — the showstopper

~3 min
Say"Here's where it earns its keep. Put a provider on PTO and the system instantly shows which appointments are impacted and proposes same-specialty, same-time reassignments — or a reschedule, or flags it for a human. Then 'apply all auto' resolves the easy ones in one click and updates every view."
8

Do it all in chat — the Workforce Assistant

~3 min
Say"Everything you just saw with clicks, the scheduler can do in plain language — and it's the same engine, so the screens stay in sync. This is an open-weights Granite model served on a GPU inside the hospital's own cloud — no third-party API, the data never leaves. Watch it act."
Then switch the demo to the PTO tab to show the chat action reflected in the UI.

Trust, platform & the future

~2 min
Say (close)"A few things make this real for a health system: every consequential action needs human approval; all data is synthetic and labeled; the models, the LLM, and the data all run on a platform you own and govern (Red Hat OpenShift AI on AWS) with full observability. For production we'd add HIPAA controls, BAAs or fully-in-VPC models, de-identification, validated + bias-reviewed models, and EHR/FHIR integration. The future: multi-agent workflows across scheduling, prior-auth, discharge and capacity — one unit today, the whole system tomorrow."
